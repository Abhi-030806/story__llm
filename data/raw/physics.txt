Motion is the change in position of an object with respect to time.

The distance travelled by an object is the total length of the path covered by the object.

Displacement is the shortest distance between the initial and final position of an object.

Speed is the distance travelled by an object per unit time.

Velocity is the displacement of an object per unit time.

Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity with time.

An object is said to be in uniform motion when it covers equal distances in equal intervals of time.

An object is said to be in non-uniform motion when it covers unequal distances in equal intervals of time.

Force is a push or pull acting on an object.

Newton's first law states that an object remains at rest or continues to move with uniform velocity unless acted upon by an external force.
